Weather and Seasons

Прочитайте электронное письмо от зарубежного друга по переписке. Напишите ответ и ответьте на его вопросы.

Subject: British weather
Hi!

The weather in London is rainy again. I don’t like autumn very much because I can’t ride my bike. My favourite season is summer.

What is the weather like in Russia now? Do you like winter? Do you play snowballs with your friends?

Hope to hear from you soon.

Best wishes,
George
В ответе нужно раскрыть вопросы:
1. What is the weather like in Russia now?
2. Do you like winter?
3. Do you play snowballs with your friends?
