Чтение. Вариант 10

На главную

London

Прочитайте текст. Для каждого предложения 1–5 выберите один правильный вариант ответа A, B или C.

Dear Josh, I'm having a brilliant time in London! I'm staying with my cousin Lucy and her family. They live in a flat in North London, not far from the tube station, so it's easy to get around the city. Lucy is the same age as me, so we have a lot of fun together. We've done all the main tourist things. We saw the Changing of the Guard at Buckingham Palace. There were so many tourists! My favourite place so far has been the Natural History Museum. It's enormous, and they have a huge skeleton of a blue whale hanging from the ceiling. We also went to the top of the London Eye. The view of the city from the giant ferris wheel is fantastic. You can see the whole of London. The weather has been typical British weather – cloudy with some rain. It rained on Tuesday, so we went to see a movie. But yesterday was sunny, and we had a picnic in Hyde Park. We fed the ducks on the lake and just relaxed on the grass. It was lovely. I love travelling on the double-decker buses. From the top deck, you feel like you're seeing the city from a different angle. Next week, Lucy's parents are taking us to see a musical in the West End. I'm so excited! We're also planning a day trip to Windsor to see the Queen's castle. I hope the weather stays nice. I'm having such a good time, but I miss you and everyone at home. Write back soon! Best wishes, Sam
1. Where is Sam staying in London?
2. What is Sam's favourite place in London so far?
3. What did Sam and Lucy do when it rained?
4. How does Sam like to travel around London?
5. What are Sam's plans for next week?

Результат

Правильные ответы: 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-B, 5-B
