A Trip to Rome

Прочитайте текст. Для каждого предложения 1–5 выберите один правильный вариант ответа A, B или C.

Dear Alex, How are you? My trip to Rome has been incredible so far. I'm here with my older sister and her friend. We arrived last Saturday and found a small, cozy hotel not far from the city center. The hotel owner is a very nice old lady who gave us a map and showed us the best places to eat real Italian pizza. Every day we walk a lot because there is so much to see. We've already visited the Colosseum and the Roman Forum. Walking through the ancient ruins, you can almost feel the history around you. My favourite place, however, is the Vatican City. We went to St. Peter's Basilica and climbed to the top of its dome. The view of Rome from up there is absolutely breathtaking, with all the orange rooftops and church domes. The food here is amazing. We eat pasta and gelato almost every day. Yesterday we tried a local specialty – pasta carbonara. It was delicious! In the evenings, we often go to small piazzas (squares) and just sit there, watching people and listening to street musicians. The atmosphere is always lively and friendly. Next weekend we are planning to take a train to visit Pompeii. I've heard it's a fascinating place – a whole ancient city buried under volcanic ash. My sister is very excited about it. I'll send you a postcard from there! Write back and tell me your news. Best wishes, Maria
1. Where is Maria spending her holidays?
2. Who is Maria travelling with?
3. What is Maria's favourite place so far?
4. What food does Maria eat almost every day?
5. What are Maria's plans for next weekend?

Результат

Правильные ответы: 1-B, 2-B, 3-C, 4-B, 5-B
