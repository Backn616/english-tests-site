Чтение. Вариант 7

На главную

Tokyo

Прочитайте текст. Для каждого предложения 1–5 выберите один правильный вариант ответа A, B или C.

Hi Lucy, I'm in Tokyo, Japan, and it's like being on another planet! Everything is so modern, clean, and efficient. I'm staying with a Japanese family to learn more about the culture. Their apartment is small but very smart, with lots of technology. They have been so kind and welcoming to me. The city is enormous. Yesterday, we went to the Shibuya Crossing. It's the busiest pedestrian crossing in the world! When the lights turn red, hundreds of people cross the street from all directions at once. It was exciting to be part of the crowd. We also visited a beautiful, quiet temple in the middle of the city. It was hard to believe the busy city was just outside. The food is delicious and looks like art. I've tried sushi from a conveyor belt restaurant, and it was so fresh. My host family also took me to a noodle shop where you eat ramen in a small booth. The broth is rich and tasty. I'm getting better at using chopsticks! In the evenings, we often walk through streets full of neon lights and tiny bars. Next weekend, my host family is taking me to see Mount Fuji. I'm so excited! We will stay in a traditional inn and hopefully get a clear view of the mountain. I'll also try to visit a manga museum. There's so much to see and do. I'm having the time of my life! Love, Chloe
1. Who is Chloe staying with in Tokyo?
2. What is special about the Shibuya Crossing?
3. Where did Chloe eat fresh sushi?
4. What is Chloe learning to use better?
5. What is Chloe's plan for next weekend?

Результат

Правильные ответы: 1-B, 2-B, 3-C, 4-A, 5-B
