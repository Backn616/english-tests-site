Чтение. Вариант 2

На главную

The Swiss Alps

Прочитайте текст. Для каждого предложения 1–5 выберите один правильный вариант ответа A, B или C.

Hello Sam, I hope you're having a good summer. I'm writing to you from a small village in the Swiss Alps. It's completely different from our busy city life. My family and I are staying in a traditional wooden chalet. From my window, I can see green mountains with snow on their tops and hear the sound of cowbells from the fields below. The weather has been perfect for hiking. Yesterday we took a cable car up to one of the mountain peaks. The ride was a bit scary but the views were worth it! At the top, there was snow, and we had a snowball fight in the middle of August. The air is so fresh and clean. We also saw some marmots – they are funny, fat little animals that whistle when they are scared. In the afternoons, we often walk down to the village. It's very small and pretty, with flowers on every balcony. We buy fresh bread and cheese from a local shop. Swiss chocolate is the best I've ever tasted! In the evenings, we play board games or read books because there's no television in our chalet. Tomorrow we are going to go paragliding. I'm a bit nervous, but my brother says it's an amazing feeling, like flying like a bird. Next week, we will move on to visit Geneva. I'm excited to see a big city again, but I will miss these quiet mountains. Take care, Tom
1. Where is Tom spending his holidays?
2. What animal sounds can Tom hear from his window?
3. What did Tom and his family do yesterday?
4. What do Tom and his family buy in the village shop?
5. How does Tom feel about his plan for tomorrow?

Результат

Правильные ответы: 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B, 5-A
