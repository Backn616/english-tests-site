Чтение. Вариант 6

На главную

Egypt

Прочитайте текст. Для каждого предложения 1–5 выберите один правильный вариант ответа A, B или C.

Hello Emma, I'm having an amazing adventure in Egypt! It's so different from Europe. I'm travelling with a group of people from my university. We flew into Cairo, a huge and chaotic city. Our hotel is right next to the Nile River. From my window, I can see feluccas (traditional boats) sailing on the water. Of course, the main reason we came here is to see the ancient monuments. Yesterday we visited the Giza Plateau. Seeing the Pyramids with my own eyes was a dream come true. They are absolutely massive. We couldn't believe that people built them so long ago without modern machines. We also saw the Sphinx, which guards the pyramids. We took a camel ride in the desert, which was a bit bumpy but fun! The heat is intense. It's over 40 degrees Celsius every day. We have to drink a lot of water and wear hats all the time. In the afternoons, when it's the hottest, we usually rest or visit the air-conditioned museum in Cairo. There, we saw the treasures of King Tutankhamun, including his famous golden mask. It's beautiful and made of solid gold. Next week, we are going on a cruise down the Nile for a few days. We will stop at different temples along the way. I'm most excited to see the temples at Luxor. I'll take lots of pictures for you! I hope you are having a good holiday too. Take care, Laura
1. Where is Laura's hotel located?
2. How did Laura and her group travel in the desert?
3. What did Laura see in the museum in Cairo?
4. What is the weather like in Egypt?
5. What are Laura's plans for next week?

Результат

Правильные ответы: 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-B, 5-A
